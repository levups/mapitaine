Benchmarks

A site of 30,000 pages in 24 languages, built in full, then again.

Building 720,000 URLs

bench/build.rb creates 30,000 records, builds their sitemap in 24 languages, builds it again with nothing new, then once more after three records changed. Every URL carries the 25 hreflang links of its page.

Ruby 3.3.6 without YJIT, Rails 8.1.4, SQLite in memory, Linux x86_64, one run
BuildTimeFiles written
Everything, 720,000 URLs31.9 s55 (17 MiB gzipped)
Nothing new0.01 s0
3 records changed1.0 s2

The largest file is 39.8 MiB before compression, under the 50 MiB the protocol allows. The URLs here are built from a string: with route helpers and a real database, the full build takes longer, and the other two change little.

Running it again

From a checkout of the gem, once with YJIT off and once with it on:

bundle exec ruby bench/build.rb
bundle exec ruby --yjit bench/build.rb

The numbers depend on the machine and the database: compare builds on the same one rather than with this table.
